Customizable CP Chair (with Clinical Input)

Oct 2024 – Present · Role: Designer / Prototyper · Team project

Figure 1 – Final rendered concept of the customizable CP chair.
1st place (design competition) Co-designed with clinicians 3D printed prototype

Project Brief & Objectives

  • Goal: Design an adjustable seating solution for children with cerebral palsy that adapts to individual postural needs.
  • Clinical input: Iterated requirements with therapists (seat depth/width, back support angles, lateral supports, headrest options).
  • Constraints: Low-cost components, modularity, ease of cleaning, compatibility with standard mobility bases.

My Role

  • Led concept generation and parametric CAD of the seat, back, and support assemblies.
  • Coordinated clinician feedback sessions and translated feedback into design changes.
  • Prepared manufacturing drawings, tolerancing, and 3D printed verification parts.

Methods & Skills
